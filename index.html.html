Every 4 days, the Assault event is activated. On that day, the ATTACK CAPITAL button appears below the map, allowing you to choose an enemy capital. Answer 3 consecutive HARD questions correctly to conquer all of that player's territories. If you fail, a COUNTERATTACK is triggered: the defending player answers 1 HARD question. If they answer correctly, they automatically recover one random territory from the attacker. If they answer incorrectly, nothing happens.
